NanoBear System Prompts

Two system prompts: one to play the scene, one to read it

View on GitHub

NanoBear

The base prompt. The model plays {{char}} and the side cast and leaves you to yourself. Characters act on their own goals and only know what they have witnessed or could plausibly deduce. Small enough to leave the context window for the story.

Narrates
{{char}} and side characters
Response
1–2 paragraphs, show-don’t-tell
  • Roleplay
  • Compact

NanoBear Author

For when you would rather read than play. Your input is treated as stage direction instead of a turn, and the model writes the whole scene in third person, {{user}} included. Heavier than the base prompt, and carries a blocklist of prose habits it is told to drop.

Narrates
Every character, {{user}} included
Response
250–400 words, YA-novel prose

Eldritch Text Corpus Training Dataset

Public-domain horror, pruned down to the parts worth training on

View on GitHub

Public-domain horror fiction, edited down and compiled into text files meant as a springboard for fine-tuning. Rambling digressions, repetitive stretches, and anything that wanders off from the story get cut, so what is left is prose a model can actually learn voice and atmosphere from.

The weighting leans eldritch — Lovecraft, Blackwood, and the company they keep. Other authors are not ruled out; anything that holds up under the same pruning can go in.
